Show notes

Long before the Phoenix Lights captivated the world, another extraordinary UFO sighting unfolded over Phoenix, Arizona. On July 7, 1947, the same day the Roswell incident made headlines, amateur photographer William A. Rhodes captured two of the earliest and most controversial UFO photographs ever taken.

His images sparked a government investigation that lasted more than five years, became known as Project Grudge Incident 40, and may have inspired one of the earliest documented Men in Black encounters. In this episode, we examine the original photographs, recently declassified government records, Project Blue Book files, and the unanswered questions that continue to surround one of the most overlooked cases in UFO history.

If you're interested in UFOs, UAPs, Project Blue Book, Roswell, government disclosure, historical UFO cases, and unexplained aerial phenomena, this investigation is one you won't want to miss.
