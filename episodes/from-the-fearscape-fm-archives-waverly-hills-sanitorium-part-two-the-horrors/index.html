Summary

The hosts discuss tuberculosis treatments and medical experiments at Waverly Hills Sanatorium in Louisville, Kentucky, along with the history of its Death Tunnel. They examine legends surrounding Room 502 and reported hauntings, including Timmy and the Creeper, and share a listener’s account of a suspected haunting.
