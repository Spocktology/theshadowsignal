Summary

Author and UFO researcher Russ Kellett discusses his claimed alien abductions, memory loss and experiences fighting in an intergalactic war. He also describes his investigation of a 1974 incident in Wales, UFO footage he recorded and his views on media coverage of UFOs.

  • 24:39Kellett discusses his claimed experiences fighting alongside aliens, and the hosts compare his account with the Shaver Mystery.
  • 31:41Kellett discusses UFO terminology and a torpedo-shaped object a friend filmed in North Wales.
  • 37:38Kellett describes UFO footage he recorded and his frustration with coverage of older cases.
  • 47:39Kellett recounts what he remembers of the alleged intergalactic war and why he believes humans were recruited.
