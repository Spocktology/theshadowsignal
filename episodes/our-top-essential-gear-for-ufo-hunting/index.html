Summary

Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart discuss tools for documenting and evaluating UFO sightings, including sky maps, flight trackers, lasers and low-light cameras. They also share cryptid reports, a UAP sighting and a listener's account of an unusual childhood experience.

Episode highlights

  • 18:08The hosts discuss Sharlie, a reported lake creature also known as Slimy Slim.
  • 26:10A reported dashcam sighting describes a light changing shape and hovering below cloud cover.
  • 35:31The hosts recommend recording observations promptly and using landmarks to help estimate an object's position.
  • 43:02Find Starlink can help observers check whether a line of lights might be satellites.
  • 50:08Sky Safari provides a map of stars, planets and satellites for identifying objects in the sky.
  • 54:12Flight-tracking apps can help check for registered aircraft near a sighting.
  • 1:03:15The hosts discuss using laser pointers to indicate where to look and caution against pointing them at aircraft.
  • 1:08:39They compare a low-light camera with a planned modification to an action camera for full-spectrum recording.
