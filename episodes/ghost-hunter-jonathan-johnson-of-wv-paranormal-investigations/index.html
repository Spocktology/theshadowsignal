Summary

Jonathan Johnson of West Virginia Paranormal Investigations discusses his experiences investigating alleged hauntings, including an unexplained scream and a 30-day investigation. He also explains how his team supports families, debunks evidence and documents investigations in its YouTube series Forgotten Relics.

Episode highlights

  • 13:54The hosts recount reports of the Fouke Monster and discuss its connection to The Legend of Boggy Creek.
  • 17:59The hosts discuss a reported UFO sighting involving multiple orbs in Rosamond, California.
  • 29:11Johnson recalls the family stories and television shows that sparked his interest in paranormal investigation.
  • 31:33Johnson describes an unexplained scream recorded during an investigation at the Haunted Hayman House.
  • 38:04Johnson explains how his group listens to families, rules out everyday causes and offers referrals for further help.
  • 1:00:47Johnson discusses the origins of Forgotten Relics and the use of location-specific objects during investigations.
  • 1:07:13Johnson describes how his team reproduced and debunked an apparent mist captured at Sweet Springs Sanitarium.
  • 1:13:04Johnson recalls investigating a house for 30 consecutive days and the lasting impact of the experience.
