Summary

Author Kevin Gibson discusses Secret Louisville, including the legend of the Witches' Tree and his visit to a cave in Tom Sawyer Park. He also describes the origins of his novel The Liberation of Crystal Hill and shares paranormal stories from his family and childhood.

Episode highlights

  • 14:51Gibson explains how Secret Louisville came about and how he researched local stories.
  • 16:14Gibson recounts the legend of the Witches' Tree in Old Louisville.
  • 25:30Gibson discusses the cave in Tom Sawyer Park, its connection to a former asylum, and his unsettling visit.
  • 32:06Gibson reflects on his uncertainty about ghosts and describes a tour of Waverly.
  • 36:40Gibson shares discoveries about Louisville history, including Shippingport Island and the former settlement called the Point.
  • 42:31Gibson explains how an award-winning short story developed into The Liberation of Crystal Hill.
  • 49:39Gibson recounts his grandmother's ghost story and his own childhood experience with a figure in his home.
  • 1:04:23The hosts read listener Alyssa Jaggers' story about a childhood encounter with a black figure.
