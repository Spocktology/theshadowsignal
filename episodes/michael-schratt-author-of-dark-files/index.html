Summary

Aviation historian and author Michael Schratt discusses his Dark Files research into alleged UFO crashes, retrievals and encounters. He describes illustrations used to reconstruct witness accounts and the difficulty of verifying reports from anonymous sources.

Episode highlights

  • 5:14Schratt describes selecting UFO cases with drawings, reports and flight paths from an archive in Chicago.
  • 6:48The conversation turns to the importance of physical evidence in UFO research.
  • 14:06Schratt presents the contested 1948 Aztec, New Mexico, UFO crash account and the research of Scott and Suzanne Ramsey.
  • 22:08Schratt introduces the Kingman UFO forced-landing account and describes the reported craft.
  • 28:49Schratt examines the unproven story that Jackie Gleason saw alien bodies at Homestead Air Force Base.
  • 37:15Schratt discusses the limits of crash-retrieval reports that protect the identities of witnesses and sources.
  • 50:51Schratt recounts a report of crates containing alleged alien bodies at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in 1953.
  • 57:21Schratt describes a 1962 account of pilots encountering an unusual craft in a hangar at Wright-Patterson.
