Summary

Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart discuss five reportedly haunted California locations: the Entity House, Lincoln Heights Jail, the Queen Mary, Hotel del Coronado, and the Winchester Mystery House. They also consider skepticism about the Winchester haunting, discuss a reported UFO sighting in Idaho, and share a listener's ghost-cat encounter.

  • 29:09The Entity House discussion covers Doris Bither's reported encounters and the investigation of her home.
  • 41:39Lincoln Heights Jail's history includes overcrowding, the Bloody Christmas incident, and reports of paranormal activity.
  • 50:05The hosts trace the Queen Mary's history and discuss reported apparitions aboard the ship.
  • 1:01:10Hotel del Coronado's history leads into a discussion of its reported haunting.
  • 1:10:12The Winchester Mystery House's unusual construction and stories about Sarah Winchester lead to questions about its alleged haunting.
