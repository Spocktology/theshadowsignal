Stefan Gearhart and guest Ed Black explore the legends surrounding Sleepy Hollow Road and Crybaby Bridge near Prospect, Kentucky. They discuss reported encounters, compare Crybaby Bridge stories from other states, and share a listener's ghost train tale.
