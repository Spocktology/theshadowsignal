Summary

Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart discuss historical UFO reports drawn from FBI files, including sightings by civilians and police officers and an account from a prisoner of war. They compare witness descriptions and discuss questions left unanswered by the records; the claims remain unverified.

Show notes

What happens when ordinary people experience something so strange they decide to report it directly to the FBI?

In this episode of The Shadow Signal, we dive into real accounts pulled from disclosure-era FBI files involving UFO sightings, unexplained encounters, and reports of humanoid beings. These are not modern internet stories or anonymous forum posts — these are firsthand statements sent to federal authorities by people who believed they witnessed something beyond conventional explanation.

From mysterious lights in the sky to disturbing close encounters with humanoid entities, these reports reveal a side of UFO history that many people have never explored.

Topics include:

- FBI UFO disclosure files

- Humanoid encounter reports

- Witness testimony and strange experiences

- Historical UFO investigations

- Government interest in unexplained phenomena

- High strangeness and contact cases

Whether you approach these stories as folklore, psychological phenomena, or genuine encounters with the unknown, the accounts themselves remain fascinating snapshots of human experience at the edge of understanding.
