Summary

The hosts explore Slenderman as a creepypasta character, comparing his appearance and abilities with figures from folklore and popular culture. They also discuss a reported triangular UFO sighting in Oklahoma and share a listener’s account of seeing shadow figures.

Episode highlights

  • 19:05A reported UFO sighting in Paoli, Oklahoma, describes a silent triangular object with three lights.
  • 26:29The hosts introduce creepypasta and compare internet stories with urban legends.
  • 28:18They describe Slenderman’s usual appearance, including his suit, blank face and elongated limbs.
  • 32:24A discussion of German folklore notes that evidence for a supposed Slenderman-like figure appears only on fan sites.
  • 48:53The hosts compare Slenderman with a faceless ghost from Japanese folklore.
  • 1:01:25They discuss variations in Slenderman’s behavior, powers and the symptoms known as Slender Sickness.
  • 1:10:23A listener named Brooke describes seeing shadow figures while recovering from surgery.
