Summary

Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart discuss witness accounts of the Phoenix Lights seen across Arizona in 1997, including descriptions of triangular formations and silent lights. They also consider flare explanations, later sightings, and available imagery.

  • 35:33The hosts begin recounting Tim Lay and his family's sighting.
  • 44:40The hosts discuss later sightings in 2007 and 2008 and a flare explanation.
