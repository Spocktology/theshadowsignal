Summary

Ed Black joins Stefan Gearhart and FearScape FM host Brad McQueary to discuss the history and reported hauntings of Whispers Estate in Mitchell, Indiana. They recount their overnight visit, including mirror gazing, unexplained sounds and other experiences they found difficult to explain.
