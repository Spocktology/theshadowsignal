Summary

The hosts examine reports of noises, moving objects and written messages associated with the Hitchings family’s home in Battersea, London, during the 1950s and 1960s. They discuss competing explanations for the alleged poltergeist activity and UFO sightings reported around the same time.

Episode highlights

  • 12:36A UFO sighting account describes a silent craft and a beam of light near Wells, Nevada.
  • 19:13The hosts introduce the Hitchings family and the discovery of an ornate key.
  • 21:57Loud noises reportedly begin in the family home and continue for weeks.
  • 26:47Witnesses report moving bed sheets, slippers and furniture, while the activity appears to follow Shirley.
  • 40:15Messages attributed to the poltergeist ask the family to contact actor Jeremy Spencer.
  • 45:20The hosts discuss the apparent end of the activity and possible explanations for the case.
  • 54:31The hosts describe a reported UFO sighting in Bexley Heath and discuss other sightings around 1956.
