Summary

Keith Age, founder of the Louisville Ghost Hunters Society, recounts an early paranormal experience and his work on Spooked and other television projects. He discusses investigating reports of hauntings, finding ordinary explanations for some claims, and experiences he says remain unexplained.

  • 34:57Age recounts an investigation where neighboring televisions helped explain a reported haunting.
  • 38:25Age describes an unexplained voice heard during a storytelling event at Post Town.
  • 44:47Age discusses filming at the house associated with the exorcism case behind The Exorcist.
