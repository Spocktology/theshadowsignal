Summary

Jeremy McGowan discusses UAPx, its research methods and its approach to collecting and sharing UAP data. He also describes updates to his O.S.I.R.I.S. project and the challenges of analyzing expedition results.

Episode highlights

  • 12:29The hosts discuss a reported UAP sighting in South Carolina.
  • 17:05Josh recounts filming an unexplained light in the New Mexico desert.
  • 22:40McGowan explains how UAPx expands on the goals of his O.S.I.R.I.S. project.
  • 26:49McGowan discusses reporting data accurately, including results that show no evidence.
  • 33:58McGowan explains why UAPx requires reliable provenance and measurement details when assessing footage.
  • 39:14The group offers advice on documenting sightings and checking possible explanations.
  • 42:37McGowan describes changes to O.S.I.R.I.S., including additional sensors and work on passive radar.
  • 55:21McGowan discusses plans to publish scientific papers and share raw data with researchers.
