Summary

Charles Hickson and Calvin Parker reported seeing a UFO and being abducted while fishing near Pascagoula, Mississippi, in 1973. The hosts discuss their account, subsequent investigations and skepticism, as well as a Bigfoot report from Mississippi around the same time.
