Summary

In 1974, Michael Taylor killed his wife Christine after an overnight exorcism conducted by church leaders in West Yorkshire. The hosts discuss the prayer group’s influence, Taylor’s acquittal on grounds of insanity, and questions about religious authority and mental health care.
