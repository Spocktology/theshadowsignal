Summary

Author and producer Dr. Lynne Kitei recounts her experiences with the Phoenix Lights, including sightings before the 1997 mass sighting and her efforts to document them. She discusses witness reports, the response from officials, the flare explanation, and the experiences of people who saw the lights.
