Show notes

Some monsters aren't supernatural.

Some are terrifyingly human. In the winter of 1977–1978, Sacramento, California, became the hunting ground of one of America's most disturbing serial killers. His name was Richard Trenton Chase, but history would remember him as The Vampire of Sacramento.

Over the course of just one month, Chase murdered six people, leaving behind crime scenes so horrifying that even seasoned investigators struggled to comprehend what they had witnessed. Driven by bizarre delusions and an obsession with blood, Chase committed acts of violence that seemed pulled straight from the pages of a horror novel.

But this wasn't fiction. This was real.
