Summary

The Spooky Crew explores ghost stories and reported Bigfoot sightings connected to Union County, Kentucky. Accounts include phantom horses, a haunted farmhouse, a woman and her dog, and a recurring hitchhiker near Nashville.

  • 18:18A farmhouse account describes moving objects, unexplained footsteps and a farmer-like apparition.
  • 21:58A Uniontown legend tells of a farm and a group of teenagers pelted with small rocks.
  • 28:36A story describes the recurring apparitions of a woman and her white dog.
