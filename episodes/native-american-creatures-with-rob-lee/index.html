Summary

Musician Rob Lee joins the hosts to discuss creatures from Native American and Inuit lore, including the Wendigo, Cherokee spirit beings and Inuit shapeshifters. Lee also talks about his four-song remix EP and plans for new music.

Episode highlights

  • 9:43The group introduces Wendigo lore from Algonquin tribes and discusses possession and cannibalism.
  • 19:33They explore descriptions of Wendigos as giants whose hunger grows as they eat.
  • 25:57The discussion turns to the Weishug, a man-eating creature or evil spirit compared with the Wendigo.
  • 34:08They describe a stone-skinned creature from Cherokee lore and a story about how villagers defeated it.
  • 38:38The hosts discuss immortal spirit people in Cherokee mythology and share a camping story about mysterious drumming.
  • 41:27They describe Raven Mockers, spirits in Cherokee folklore said to prey on the sick and dying.
  • 47:26The group discusses elusive Inuit shapeshifters and stories of people becoming disoriented.
  • 55:16Rob Lee talks about his remix EP, lost recordings and plans for new music.
