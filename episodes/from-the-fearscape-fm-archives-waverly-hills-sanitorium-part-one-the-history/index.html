Summary

Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart trace the history of Waverly Hills Sanatorium in Louisville, from the origins of its name to its role in treating tuberculosis. They also discuss the building’s later use as a geriatric center, abandoned redevelopment plans, and restoration efforts.

Episode highlights

  • 19:05The property’s early ownership and the school that inspired the Waverly name.
  • 23:32Tuberculosis, its transmission, and the need for treatment in Jefferson County.
  • 24:32The opening of the original wooden sanatorium and the use of fresh air as a treatment.
  • 30:53Growing demand for beds and the construction of the five-story hospital.
  • 34:56The building’s later use as Woodhaven Geriatric Center and its closure amid patient neglect.
  • 37:55An unsuccessful proposal to turn the property into a medium-security prison.
  • 41:34A proposed religious attraction featuring a giant statue and why the project was scrapped.
  • 45:03Tina and Charlie Mattingly’s purchase of the property and efforts to restore it.
