Summary

Keith Age joins Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart to discuss the reported exorcism of Roland Doe and its connection to The Exorcist. They recount accounts from the case, Age's documentary experience, and skeptical interpretations of the reported events.

Episode highlights

  • 5:04The hosts discuss a reported sighting of a large, red-eyed creature near O'Hare.
  • 12:50The discussion turns to the events surrounding Roland Doe in 1949 and the role of his aunt Harriet.
  • 16:27The hosts recount reported scratching sounds, unexplained water and movement in the family's Maryland home.
  • 24:09They describe a reported exorcism attempt by Father E. Albert Hughes and the family's move toward St. Louis.
  • 33:39Age recalls visiting a location connected to the case while filming the documentary.
  • 43:30The group discusses the priests' diary and an account of a janitor seeing Roland levitate.
  • 52:13A skeptic's challenges to supernatural claims and details of the case are considered.
  • 1:02:19The hosts discuss skeptic Joe Nickell's alternative explanations for reported phenomena.
