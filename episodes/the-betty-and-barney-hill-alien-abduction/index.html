Summary

Santosh David joins Josh and Stefan to discuss Betty and Barney Hill's 1961 account of a UFO sighting and alleged abduction. They examine the couple's missing time, hypnotic regression sessions, reported memories and the case's influence on later abduction stories.

  • 29:38Betty's nightmares and the couple's decision to undergo hypnotic regression.
