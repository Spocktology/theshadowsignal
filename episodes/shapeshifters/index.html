Summary

Josh Rutledge and Stefan Gearhart discuss shapeshifters in folklore and mythology, including Greek, British, Irish, Norse and Navajo traditions. They also share a personal account of unsettling experiences and read a listener's story about an allegedly haunted Kentucky home.
