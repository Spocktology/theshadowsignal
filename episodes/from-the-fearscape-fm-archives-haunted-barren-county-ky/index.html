Summary

Stefan Gearhart and guest Ed Black explore ghost stories and creature legends from Barren County, Kentucky, including the Headless Woman of Keno and the Slash Monster. They discuss reported Bigfoot sightings, a newspaper account of a supposedly haunted tree, and a listener's family ghost story.

Episode highlights

  • 19:06A 1905 newspaper story describes a dying man who said he was haunted by a woman he had murdered.
  • 22:03A group near Glasgow reports seeing a house that vanished as they approached.
  • 25:59The legend of the Headless Woman of Keno includes several sightings by the Bunch family.
  • 30:29Stories associated with Slash Bridge feature a headless rider and unexplained sounds.
  • 36:17The Slash Monster is described as a Sasquatch-like legend, followed by a proposed explanation for its origins.
  • 42:45A reported Barren County Bigfoot sighting describes a tall, black-haired figure crossing a road.
  • 50:17A 1907 newspaper account offers an insect-related explanation for rainfall beneath a supposedly haunted tree.
  • 55:50A listener shares a family story about a comforting apparition after a mother's death.
