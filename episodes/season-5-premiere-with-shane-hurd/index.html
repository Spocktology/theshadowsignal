Summary

Author and MUFON Experiencer Resource Team member Shane Hurd explains how the team supports people who report contact experiences and how its work differs from UFO investigations. The conversation also covers cattle deaths, challenges in reporting and investigating sightings, and the role of perception in interpreting unusual experiences.
