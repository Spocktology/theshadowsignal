Summary

Author and podcast host Ryan Sprague recounts the childhood UFO sighting that sparked his interest in the phenomenon. He discusses the impact of sightings on witnesses, a family's account of a triangular craft and subsequent disturbances, and his approach to listening to experiencers.

Episode highlights

  • 22:33Sprague discusses the Phoenix Lights and their place in his book.
  • 26:03Sprague recounts his 1995 sighting of triangular lights over the St. Lawrence River.
  • 30:45Sprague describes how UFO sightings can affect witnesses and why their experiences afterward matter to him.
  • 33:05The group discusses possible connections between UFO sightings and other reported paranormal activity.
  • 36:54Sprague shares a Michigan family's differing perceptions of a triangular craft and the disturbances they reported afterward.
  • 45:38Sprague explains his approach to interviewing witnesses without telling them what happened.
  • 55:46Sprague reflects on places his research has taken him and his wish to visit Rendlesham Forest.
