Summary

Stefan Gearhart and guest host Ed Black discuss the Bell Witch legend, including the alleged haunting of the Bell family in Tennessee and stories associated with the Bell Witch Cave. They also read accounts of unusual experiences at the property and discuss the legend’s influence on films.

Episode highlights

  • 8:15Stefan introduces the Bell Witch as a Tennessee destination he hopes to visit.
  • 13:09Stefan describes an unsettling experience during a tour in Tombstone.
  • 27:21The hosts recount the Bell family’s arrival in Tennessee and the strange events said to have occurred on their farm.
  • 30:05They discuss the alleged torment of Betsy and John Bell and the witch’s objection to Betsy’s engagement.
  • 33:54The discussion turns to the Bell Witch Cave and a story about a child rescued inside it.
  • 41:16The hosts discuss accounts of John Bell’s death, a later book about the haunting, and films associated with the legend.
  • 46:12They read Rick White’s account of strange experiences connected to filming near the cave.
  • 55:17The hosts read and question Willie Windwalker’s account of an investigation at the cave.
